# 🚀 UNOVARA

### AI-powered social network connecting people with opportunities.

![Unovara Banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400)

## 🌍 Vision

Unovara is a next-generation social platform designed to connect people, intelligence, and opportunities.

Unlike traditional social networks that focus mainly on content sharing, Unovara helps people discover:
- 💼 Career opportunities
- 🤝 Business connections
- 📚 Learning resources
- 🌟 Personal growth opportunities

Our mission is to create a world where everyone can discover opportunities based on their skills, interests, and goals.

---

# 💡 The Problem

Millions of people use social media every day, but many struggle to find:
- The right jobs
- Business partners
- Learning opportunities
- Communities that match their skills

Social networks connect people, but they rarely connect people with opportunities.

---

Unovara combines:
